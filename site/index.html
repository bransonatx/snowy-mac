Before you open it

This is an early test build. Apple has not checked it yet, so your Mac asks you to allow it once.

  1. Open the download and drag Seasons into Applications.
  2. Open Seasons. Your Mac says it can’t check the app. Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.

After that it opens like any other app. Look for the leafsnowflake in your menu bar.
